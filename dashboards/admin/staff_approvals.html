🧪 Testing Guide — Staff Hour Approvals

What this page is: This is where you review hours that staff members have submitted. You decide if those hours count or not.

The table — Shows all submitted shifts. By default it shows only Pending ones (waiting for your decision).
Approve — Click Approve to accept the hours. The staff member will earn points for those hours.
Reject — Click Reject if the hours look wrong or should not count.
Search — Type a name or badge number to find a specific person's submissions.

How to test: Make sure a staff member has submitted hours first, then find their name here and click Approve or Reject.
