🧪 Testing Guide — Volunteer Dashboard

What this page is: This is your personal dashboard as a volunteer. From here you can log your hours and track your points.

Start Time — Click this when you begin your shift. The clock starts running.
End Time — Click this when your shift is over. Your hours will be saved and sent to an admin for approval.
Notes — Optionally describe what you worked on before clicking End Time.
My Points — Shows your total points earned from approved hours.
My Submitted Hours — Shows every individual shift you have submitted.

How to test: Click Start Time, wait a moment, then click End Time. Add a note if you like. Then check My Submitted Hours to see it appear as Pending.
